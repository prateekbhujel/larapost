Social publishing infrastructure for Laravel

Connect only the networks you need, publish immediately or on a schedule, and optionally expose the same workflow to MCP clients without handing your social credentials to an AI provider.

Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok

Enable one platform, several, or all of them. Disabled drivers stay out of the runtime surface.

Queues and schedules

Persist first, avoid duplicate claims, retry failures, and let Laravel's scheduler and queue do the operational work.

What LaraPost is for

LaraPost is not a separate social media SaaS. It is infrastructure that lives inside your Laravel application. Your application keeps the account records, provider tokens, publishing history, queue configuration, and business rules.

That makes it useful for SaaS products, CRMs, content systems, marketplaces, agency tools, and internal applications that need social publishing as a product capability rather than another browser tab.

Installation

composer require prateekbhujel/larapost
php artisan larapost:install

The installer asks which platforms you want and whether you want the optional MCP server. Nothing requires you to configure every provider.

A minimal publishing example

use SocialSync\Facades\SocialMedia;

SocialMedia::post()
    ->content('We just shipped a new release.')
    ->platform('facebook')
    ->publish();
