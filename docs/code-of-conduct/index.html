Code of Conduct

Community expectations for respectful, constructive participation in LaraPost.

Our standards

Positive participation includes respectful and constructive feedback, openness to different viewpoints, and assuming good intent while asking clarifying questions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, trolling, intimidation, insulting comments, or publishing private information without permission.

Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Reporting

Report incidents privately to prateekbhujelpb@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
