Community Standards

Code of Conduct

The LaraPost community is committed to a respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone.

Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

  • Constructive and respectful feedback
  • Openness to different viewpoints
  • Assuming good intent and clarifying before escalating

Unacceptable behavior includes:

  • Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
  • Trolling, intimidation, and insulting language
  • Publishing private information without consent

Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Reporting

Report incidents privately to prateekbhujelpb@gmail.com.

All reports are handled promptly and confidentially.
